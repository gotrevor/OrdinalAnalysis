## The two structural findings (details in PORT-V434.md)

1. **The one-sided LK calculus changed shape, not just names.**  `Sequent L` went
   `List (Proposition L)` → `LK.Sequent L = Multiset …`, and the single subset rule
   `contraction : ⊢ Δ → Δ ⊆ Γ → ⊢ Γ` split into `weakening` + a two-into-one `contraction`.  Not
   chaseable through call sites: this development is a recursion that reads the principal formula
   off the *head* of a sequent, and `BoundedDerivable`/`OmegaDerivable`/`IDerivable`/`ACA.Derivation`
   are all list-indexed.  Resolution: `OrdinalAnalysis/Compat.lean` (list sequents, `ˡ⁺`, the
   coercion lemmas, `provable_iff_list`), `OrdinalAnalysis/CompatSO.lean` (second-order copy) and
   `OrdinalAnalysis/FinLK.lean` — the list calculus `⊢ᶠ¹` plus the **proved** translations
   `FinDerivation.ofDerivation : ⊢ᴸᴷ¹ Δ → (Γ : Sequent L) → ↑Γ = Δ → ⊢ᶠ¹ Γ`,
   `FinDerivation.toUpstream`, and `isCutFree_ofDerivation`.  Nothing is axiomatised, and the
   headline theorems still hang off upstream's own `⊢ᴸᴷ¹`: `Proof/Bridge.lean` exposes
   `cutFree_of_upstreamDerivation` and `toUpstreamDerivation`.
2. **A family of proofs now diverges in memory instead of failing.**  A full `simp` that unfolds a
   `𝚺₁.Semisentence` or a `PR.Blueprint`, or an anonymous constructor against a syntax-recursive
   predicate (`PositiveIn`, `LevelBounded`) at a *concrete* formula, explores the blueprint/`lMap`
   machinery behind it and is OOM-killed **with no error printed** (13 GB on a 178-line file).
   157 sites were fixed by script (`simp only [<def>, val_mkSigma]`, then the original simp) and
   five by hand.  W5b/W7 carry the bisect recipe, which matters precisely because a diverging `simp`
   prints nothing.
